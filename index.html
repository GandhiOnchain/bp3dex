Hidden BasePaint History

You might've stumbled across a piece of hidden BasePaint history!

On early canvases, the smart contract didn't strictly enforce official canvas boundaries. Some artists discovered this loophole and secretly painted thousands of pixels outside the official border!

Because this 3D engine pulls raw data directly from the blockchain, turning on Hidden Art bypasses the official cropping and reveals these secret, out-of-bounds drawings that you won't see anywhere else.
